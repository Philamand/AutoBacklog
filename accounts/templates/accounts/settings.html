How to import your public library?

  • In your PlayStation account settings, make sure that your library is public and that your account is searchable by all.
  • Enter your PlayStation username in a case-sensitive manner.
  • Wait for your library to finish loading (it may take a few minutes).

How to get your NPSSO ?

  • In your web browser, access https://store.playstation.com and log in with your PSN account.
  • In the same browser, access https://ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie
  • You should see a response with npsso followed by a string of letters and numbers. Highlight and copy this (without the "").

How to get your JSON file?

  • Get your NPSSO token by following the instructions above.
  • Download our open-source python script.
  • Follow the instruction from the README.
  • Import the generated json file and click "Sync with json file".
