Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 01-01-2025

1. Data We Collect

We only collect what's necessary to operate the app:

  • Account Information: Email address.
  • PlayStation Data: NPSSO token (to fetch your game library and trophies via Sony's API).
  • Game Library: Titles in your library and their related status (bought, played, finished...).
  • Session Cookies: Essential for app functionality (no tracking).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the service: Display your backlog, trophy progress and shelving status.
  • To improve the app: Anonymized usage stats via Umami Analytics (hosted on our server in France, no cookies are used).
  • To communicate: Critical app updates.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

  • Never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Stored securely on our server in France.

4. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: by emailing us.
  • Export your data: request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • Revoke access: Disconnect your NPSSO token any time from the settings or by emailing us.

5. Cookies

We only use strictly necesarry session cookies. We don't use any tracking/advertising cookies.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted here. Continued use means you accept them.
