About AutoBacklog

AutoBacklog is a backlog service that let you connect your PlayStation Network account to automatically import your library and tag them accordingly: bought, played, finished, platinumed.

I built AutoBacklog after subscribing to PlayStation+ Premium: my library became a mess ! Impossible to know what games I finished, abandonned, downloaded but never opened or even to differenciate between games I bought, games I got as a monthly game and games from the game catalog.

Alpha Notice

The service is still in it's very early stage, so you should expect bugs and missing features. If you wish to report a bug or request a feature, please use the "Feedback" page (need to be logged in) or send an email to the address below.
